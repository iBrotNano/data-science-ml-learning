# 🍓 **Strawberry Ice Cream – English Translation**

## Ingredients

- 2 cups fresh strawberries  
- 1 cup heavy cream  
- ½ cup milk  
- ½ cup sugar  
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice  

## Instructions

1. Wash the strawberries and purée them.  
2. Stir in the sugar until it dissolves.  
3. Add the milk and lemon juice.  
4. Whip the cream until stiff and gently fold it into the strawberry mixture.  
5. Pour the mixture into a bowl and freeze for 4–6 hours.  
6. Stir every 30 minutes to keep the texture creamy.
